# SAP MM — Trainer Track (S/4HANA Sourcing & Procurement)

Goal: trainer-ready competence in SAP S/4HANA MM + C_TS452 certification.
Pace: ~5 h/day, 5 days/week → 8 weeks (~180 h). Each module = learn → practise in system → build trainer kit.

| # | Module | Week | Hours | Trainer kit output |
|---|--------|------|-------|--------------------|
| 1 | Foundations & Organisational Structure | 1 | 15 | Org-structure diagram + navigation cheat sheet |
| 2 | Master Data I — Business Partner (Supplier) | 1–2 | 15 | Supplier creation job aid |
| 3 | Master Data II — Material/Product, Info Records, Source Lists | 2 | 20 | Product master field guide |
| 4 | The Procure-to-Pay Cycle (end-to-end) | 3 | 25 | P2P demo scenario + learner workbook |
| 5 | Purchasing in depth — PRs, RFQs, POs, Contracts, Scheduling Agreements | 3–4 | 20 | Purchasing exercises pack |
| 6 | Inventory Management — Goods Movements, Movement Types, Stock Types | 4–5 | 20 | Movement-type quick reference |
| 7 | Logistics Invoice Verification & Integration with FI | 5 | 15 | Invoice-matching exercises |
| 8 | Pricing, Release Strategies & Output | 6 | 15 | Approval workflow demo |
| 9 | MRP Basics, Special Procurement & Valuation | 6–7 | 15 | MRP walkthrough |
| 10 | Reporting, Fiori Analytics & Exam Revision | 7–8 | 20 | Mock exams + full course handbook |

## Each module contains
- Lessons (read or listen — Kokoro audio)
- "Try it in the system" tasks (Fiori app + SAP GUI transaction)
- Flashcards and quiz
- Trainer notes: how to teach it, common learner mistakes, demo script

## Required alongside
- Practice system (IDES / S/4HANA access rental or SAP CAL trial)
- learning.sap.com — "Business Processes in SAP S/4HANA Sourcing & Procurement"
